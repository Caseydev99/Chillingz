About Chillingz

Chillingz helps you find something good to do — matched to your mood, your interests and where you are.

What we do

London has thousands of restaurants, games, gigs, classes and meetups. Chillingz brings them together in one place, so you can stop scrolling and start going out.

Tell us what you're into, and we'll suggest places and events that fit. Save your favourites and share them with friends — on the website or in the Chillingz app.

Chillingz is a discovery and referral platform: venues and organisers run the experiences, and you book with them directly. Read more in our terms.

How it works

  1. 1. Discover

    Browse by category, search, or follow one of our guides.

  2. 2. Save

    Tap the heart to keep a shortlist. It follows you to the app.

  3. 3. Go

    Book directly with the venue or get tickets from the organiser.
