Cookie policy

This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage the Chillingz website uses. You can change your choice at any time with the button (also in the footer of every page). For how we handle personal data generally, see our privacy policy.

Essential storage (always on)

These make the website work and don't track you across other sites. Under UK rules (PECR) they don't need your consent.

Analytics cookies (only if you accept)

If you click "Accept all", we may use analytics cookies to understand how the site is used so we can improve it. If you click "Decline", none are set. The website doesn't currently load any analytics, and we'll update this page before we add any.

Other companies' content

  • Google Fonts — the website's font is downloaded from Google, which receives your IP address to send it. No cookies are set.
  • Google Maps — maps on venue pages only load when you press "Show map". Google may then set its own cookies; see Google's cookie policy.
  • Links to venues and ticket sites — when you leave Chillingz, those sites' own cookie policies apply.

Controlling cookies

You can change your choice with the cookie settings button above, and delete stored data in your browser's settings. Deleting it logs you out and clears any favourites saved as a guest.
